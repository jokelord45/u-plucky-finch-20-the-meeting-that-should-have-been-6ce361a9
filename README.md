One line, 118 characters: **"After an hour, the meeting's only decision was to schedule another meeting to decide what the first meeting was about."**

The joke lands on the recursion, not on the obvious complaint: an hour of people's time produces nothing except the decision to spend more people's time figuring out what the first hour was for. "After an hour" sets the scene in three words, "only decision" is the sting, and the turn arrives with the last words — the meeting exists only to justify itself. Anyone who has sat through a follow-up scheduled to debrief a meeting that solved nothing recognises it instantly.
